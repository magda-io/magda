## Reporting rules fixed before outcomes

1. **Forced conclusion C**, regardless of results. No A/B, promotion or production adoption claim.
2. A point graded metric is undefined when any U can affect its complete-corpus IDCG or exact-identity precision—even if the unknown record was not retrieved. Do not silently omit unknowns from IDCG or count them as irrelevant.
3. Show fully validated query/intent-family comparisons and all known exact-match regressions per seed. Label any reduced-family mean **descriptive**, with its actual cohort count; it is not the preregistered eight-family primary metric.
4. Optional sensitivity enumerates possible ordinal grades0,1,2,3 for explicitly uncertain intent/document units, correlating paraphrases appropriately and recomputing full IDCG each time. Bounds are label-sensitivity assumptions, not confidence intervals or a basis for choosing a system. Disclose any enumeration cap rather than silently collapsing unknowns.
5. Keep ambiguous/no-answer diagnostics separate. Surface returned IDs and uncertainty; do not manufacture ordinary zero-NDCG wins.
6. Retain all three seeds, failures, correctness/latency evidence and exact-target displacement. Never average away a seed regression. Matched-boost comparisons isolate boost changes, but normalized-architecture contrasts also include root/child family restructuring and are not pure causal processor effects.
7. No query, label, metadata, mask, weight or candidate selection changes after outcomes. A needed post-outcome correction invalidates the registered replay and must be reported, not quietly rerun.
