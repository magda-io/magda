## Source honesty and independence

- These are analyst-authored information needs, **not real user logs, user quotations or validated user requests**. Public government/research sources motivate plausible analytical purposes. Chosen dates, localities and acceptance constraints are authored unless the per-task basis explicitly says otherwise.
- Web search was followed by fetch of each source. A successful HTTP status does not imply a substantive body was returned: truncation, title-only responses, PDF extraction failure, bot challenges and HTTP 403 are disclosed per task. Search snippets are not represented as fetched article content.
- Conservative provenance classification: **8 constructed external questions** and **7 catalogue-derived supplements** (3 objective lookups plus 4 externally documented products). The four product-informed supplements are q04, q09, q11 and q12. They were not selected from the frozen Magda corpus, but product documentation can still privilege answerable topics; they are not counted as independent external-question tasks.
- The eight external-question slots are q02, q03, q05, q08, q10, q13, q14 and q15: three development, five heldout. No legitimately sourced real-log tasks were available.
- Selection bias remains in every stratum: Australian topics and author-selected years, accessible sources, metadata-rich public products, special-case exact lookups, and suspected fine-grained coverage gaps. A majority external panel is not representative of catalogue users or production query frequencies.
- The author did **not** read system ranking outputs, prototype/query-adapter/evaluation-metrics files, or older logs; did not issue relevance queries to live Search; did not inspect evaluation results or tune weights. No production edit or public mutation was performed. No MAGDA catalogue CLI was invoked.
- **Exposure exception disclosed immediately to the experiment owner:** the explicitly permitted legacy public sample was opened once for metadata and returned incidental score/quality fields alongside the first known record. They were not used for query/target selection or analysis. The read stopped; the author requested a stripped metadata projection and did not continue reading the raw sample. The observed topic was Flow-MER river oxygen logging; no discovery task uses that topic. Therefore an absolute claim that the author has never seen any score field would be false, although the author has not seen system comparison outputs or used score values. This is an imperfect information barrier, not hidden pristine blinding.

## Acceptance and uncertainty

Every record states geography, time, variables, granularity and format/access as hard requirements; preferences and explicit competing-but-unsuitable cases are separate. Unspecified dimensions are intentionally unspecified rather than silently supplied from a guessed target. A record may support partial relevance without satisfying every hard requirement; full suitability requires supported evidence. **Use `U` for insufficient metadata**, including undocumented distribution contents, coordinate system, dates, privacy/access, temporal resolution or required variables. Do not equate unknown with irrelevant, or infer downloadable contents from a promising title/filename.

q14 and q15 request only public **aggregates**, not unavailable private data. Their `missing_answer` category records a **plausible absence hypothesis**, not established absence. A stratified-sample miss says nothing about live catalogue/global absence. Before treating either as an objective negative, perform the recorded exhaustive metadata-only corpus audit and record unresolved candidates as U. A zero-relevance retrieval pool or failed live search cannot verify absence. No such absence audit has yet been completed by this author.
