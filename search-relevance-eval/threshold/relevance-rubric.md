## Semantic algorithm: all8 families

For each intent/record, apply the following ordered rules, then compare against its explicit planned grade:

1. Product AND process AND observed-not-simulated basis must meet the explicit query. A contradicted or different product/process is0, even with many shared words. Missing evidence or an uncertain relation is U, never automatic0. Required scope is stated in all paraphrases: potable municipal water; city street trees; electric-vehicle battery cells; migratory birds tracked at night by radar; public road bus boarding points for wheelchair access; fresh fish intended as food in refrigerated spoilage testing; textile dye wastewater treatment for color removal; residential heat pumps acoustically tested under operating load. No hidden factory, site, format, time period, causal or use-case restriction may be introduced.
2. When scope and basis match, count supported measurement facts from exactly the three frozen facts:3=>grade3 direct,2=>grade2 useful,1=>grade1 tangential,0=>grade0. A missing measurement variable is not the same as denying an intrinsic product property. Product/process words alone cannot imply a measurement was collected. A full answer needs all three, but two documented requested facts are materially useful partial evidence.
3. No additional file-format restriction applies to semantic queries; declared CSV/JSON availability is a fixture-integrity constraint, not an unstated user requirement. Never grade a file format as hard0 unless explicitly requested in a future separately preregistered task. Here no such future tasks may be added after freeze.
4. Canonical fact evidence can occur in approved primary or nested fields. Root anchor's required measurements must be absent from nested searchable text; nested anchor's required measurements must be absent from dataset primary fields. Dataset product/process/basis remains explicit in both. Do not rewrite production title+description projections to change branch similarity.
