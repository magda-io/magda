## Initial audit findings from the prior study

1. **Hidden restrictions created false negatives.** Broad orchard, confectionery, drink and factory wording did not entail cacao/chocolate/dairy/production-machinery restrictions. Simulation exclusion, CSV, numerical range or traceability cannot be hidden in intent metadata. Every submitted paraphrase must actually express the restriction used to label otherwise plausible records zero. Conversely an anchor must support every requirement expressed by the query.
2. **Ontological attributes are not independently omissible measurements.** Product/process cannot simultaneously entail and prohibit an attribute merely to manufacture grade 2/1. Count independently documented measurements, not missing generator tags. Administrative observations must not become fictitious physical trials. Simulation-only records must not acquire observation labels through title, distribution or summary wording.
3. **Legacy validation never strictly passed.** The [diagnostic document](<../synthetic/diagnostic-validation.json>) reports 40,488 known of 40,500 query–record–seed pairs, `full_validation_passed: false`, no promotion and forced legacy recommendation C. These remain historical facts, not a fresh threshold decision.
4. **Preserve all twelve original U pairs.** The [mask](<../synthetic/auditoutputs/diagnostic-uncertainty-mask-v1.jsonl>) contains a057 × {s08,d03} × three seeds, expanding to s08's three paraphrases plus d03's one query: 12 query–record–seed labels. Grounds-tool sanitation associated with a confectionery factory has the measurements; broad factory-hygiene wording does not exclude it. Changing its site/process or quietly choosing the factory-versus-landscaping interpretation to force zero is not repair against unchanged truth. Do not report a passed “primary eight” by dropping the disputed intent and concealing the original failure.
5. **Mechanical checks are not semantic certification.** Literal quotations, complete matrices, matched hashes and generator self-checks are necessary but not proof of entailment. Independent actual-text observations and defensible query interpretation remain mandatory.
6. **Full review can be reused, not invented.** The legacy [plan](<../synthetic/validation-plan.md>) records 540 actual-text reviews and full-row byte-equality reuse. New code may verify that closure without repeating every unchanged read. Old labels, snippets or a manifest status alone cannot certify new query meanings, changed metadata or new growth records.

## Growth and independence constraints

Growth membership and prefix order are fixed before target embeddings or results. If accidentally relevant, report a frozen-label contradiction before retrieval; do not remove it because a model retrieves it. Review growth exact IDs for target aliases/collisions as well.

Reusing existing independently validated core records as growth distractors is permissible only when source records are independently negative for the new held needs and the preregistration makes the mapping explicit. A corpus must not contain the same existing core record twice under cloned unique IDs: uniqueness is not independence. If a physical record participates in both core and growth roles across different experiments, disclose that relation and preserve nonoverlapping membership within each corpus. Pure ID-renaming reuse needs explicit deterministic mapping and identity safety checks; changing searchable fields or ordinary metadata defaults requires new actual-text review. Report the number of unique source texts and the limitation of any deliberate duplicates.
