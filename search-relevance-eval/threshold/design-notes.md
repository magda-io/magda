## Why the registered gates are conservative

.45 is the fixed primary; .48/.46 form the ONLY DEV-selectable safer alternative, stricter first. Every heldout threshold is reported, but no heldout candidate selection. Minimum+.05 useful Recall@10 plus recovered targets in at least two heldout families is required at BOTH1000 and2000 in EACH seed, ensuring benefit beyond a single easy query at the registered catalog-growth scales. Core60 instead requires Recall delta>=0, Success@5 delta>=0 and NDCG delta>=-.02, without a gain/recovery quota. This pre-outcome design correction prevents a tiny-catalog baseline ceiling from falsely forcing retention despite useful growth-scale gains. DEV selection analogously requires+.03 Recall and one recovered DEV family at BOTH growth levels per seed, with core60 Recall noninferiority0. NDCG/Success/per-family/exact/FP/correctness/latency/growth guards remain at ALL3 mandatory sizes, no pooled rescue. B utility-failure reasoning applies only where the gain bar is registered (1000/2000), never merely core60 lacking gain.

Exact displacement/wrong-identity introduction and authorization exposure are absolute blockers, not a weighted score. False-positive limits count additions instead of net counts so substitutions cannot hide new mistakes. Mean top10 additions≤.25/query and at most1/query, tighter new top5 bounds and no new named-hard-negative/OR-control top5 exposure are a deliberate small manual-review-risk budget. All-negative query classes have incremental new-ID top5 bounds (unrelated mean≤.5/each≤1; OR-lookalike mean≤1/each≤2), not absolute ceilings on unchanged baseline lexical noise. This pre-outcome attribution correction prevents unchanged production keyword-OR mistakes from masquerading as costs of a threshold-only change. Report absolute totals for BOTH systems as diagnostics; never hide them. Existing keyword eligibility does NOT exempt a grade0 ID newly promoted into candidate topK: that is still a new rank exposure. These are preregistered engineering risk limits, not empirically estimated user tolerances.

Growth stability uses differences of PAIRED treatment changes, not candidate-only crowding: delta_M(s)=M(candidate,s)-M(.50,s), with delta_Recall(growth)-delta_Recall(core60)>=-.02 and delta_NDCG(growth)-delta_NDCG(core60)>=-.03 at BOTH1000/2000 in EACH seed. Growth FP caps apply to E(growth)-E(core60), where E is the count of NEW candidate top10 grade0 IDs absent matched-size baseline top10: mean increase≤.25 and each query≤1, separately on semantic and negative populations. This pre-outcome attribution correction prevents intrinsic catalog crowding shared by both systems from being mislabeled threshold cost. Absolute baseline/candidate counts and Recall/NDCG levels and each system's catalog-size changes remain fully reported. Every same-level incremental FP, +.05 utility/recovery, noninferiority, exact/security/latency guard remains intact. Growth grade1 is never mislabeled false-positive; full IDCG includes it. Ten measured repetitions (plus one warmup) are fixed for the grid: heldsemantic180/combinedheldpositive270/allnegative120 suffice for their declared p95 groups. Heldexact90 and DEVpositive90 report descriptive interpolated p95 with guard_eligible:false, not a confirmatory p95 guard; exact rank/precision judgments remain fully valid. Do not inflate repetitions after observing timing or silently pool seeds.

The [.40,.52] opportunity band and [.45,.50) crossing interval are diagnosis of whether immutable evidence can test this hypothesis, not generation acceptance targets. Native root/nested branch scores are assessed for a complete frozen72-pair/seed set only after immutable data and DEV selection record. Too few opportunities =>D, no heldout rerolls. Fixed mixed-axis pairs identify which cutoff branch matters; they never become primary candidates after outcomes. Topk10 is a diagnostic, not a radial substitute or complete vector-recall oracle.
